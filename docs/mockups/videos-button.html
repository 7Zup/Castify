"N videos found" must never cover the site

Today the floating button sits over the bottom right of the page and hides the site's own buttons (episodes, settings, fullscreen). Three ways to keep it reachable without covering anything. The page below is a site's video player with its controls.

A · In the top bar (recommended)

A video icon next to Cast, with a red count badge. Tap → the video list. Nothing over the page, ever. Grey without badge when no video is found.
