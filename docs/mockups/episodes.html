TV shows: change episode / season, Next, Auto next

Shown only when Castify recognizes a series episode (address like …/tv/<id>/<season>/<episode>). Episode names and pictures come from a public TV database (no account). Picking an episode: the phone loads it hidden, finds its video and sends it to the TV, like a normal cast. Auto next is off by default, the same setting on the phone and the TV.

Phone · full remote

P1 · Episodes in the remote

Under the controls: the season picker, then the season's episodes (current one marked). Tap an episode → it plays on the TV. Auto next checkbox at the bottom.
