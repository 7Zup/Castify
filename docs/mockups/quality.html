Change the video quality while it plays

Same pattern as subtitles. The qualities are the ones the site offers for this video (best first). Picking one switches without restarting the video. A video with one quality only: the button is greyed out and the list says "Only one quality for this video". When an episode changes (phone, TV, Next, Auto next), the best quality is picked by default.
