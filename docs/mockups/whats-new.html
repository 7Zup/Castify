Updates: checked on the TV home, then "What's new"

1. Update check. The TV checks for an update every time its home screen shows (when it comes back to the front, after a video ends or stops), not only when the app really starts: on a Chromecast the app is rarely closed. At most once every 10 minutes, nothing on screen while it checks (T0 is only an option). The phone keeps checking at each launch.
2. What's new. The first time a new version opens, a short pop-up says what changed for the user, in plain words (2–4 items, no technical terms), with OK. Notes are written with each release and shipped inside the app (they show offline).
3. Every version. All the notes, newest first, on the phone from the ⋮ menu (P2–P3), and on the TV from the version label of the home screen (T2).
